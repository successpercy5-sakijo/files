Our Services

Dr. Benjamin Mbhiko specializes in general practice (GP) healthcare, addressing a broad range of patient needs for all adult age groups

🩺

Preventive care

Routine health check-ups, health screenings, and general wellness assessments.

💊

Acute illness management

Diagnosis and treatment of short-term illnesses or infections.

📈

Chronic disease management

Ongoing care for conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, and arthritis.

🤰

Antenatal and maternal health support

Pregnancy monitoring, prenatal check-ups, and guidance on maternal health, including ultrasound services.

🧪

Diagnostic services

Blood tests, basic laboratory investigations, and other routine diagnostic procedures.

👂

Audiology consultations

Hearing assessments and related services.

⚕️

Male Circumcision

Male circumcision is offered exclusively during the month of June.
